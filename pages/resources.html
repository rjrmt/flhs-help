How do I get a laptop?

Pick up at the Media Center. Bring your Canvas approval message.

  1. 1 Parent signs the 1:1 laptop agreement in Focus Parent Portal.
  2. 2 Wait for your Canvas approval message (required to pick up).
  3. 3 Go to the Media Center during pickup hours.

Pickup hours · Before school 7:00–7:40 AM, or during study hall with a teacher pass.
